Experimental use and compliance notice

V27 is In Testing. This template is an investigation and planning aid, not a sole source of truth for licensing, security, privacy, legal, compliance, personnel, or procurement decisions. Coverage depends on licensing, onboarding, connectors, retention, permissions, and configured app/domain lists.

Risk scores and anomalies are heuristic triage signals. File proximity is temporal correlation, not proof of upload, disclosure, misuse, or data leakage. Customers are responsible for lawful collection, notices and consent, access controls, sensitivity labels, retention, data residency, and compliance. This public template does not upload customer data to this repository; customers control collection, storage, deployment, and sharing.
